Dragon Ball Z filler FAQ

How many filler episodes does Dragon Ball Z have?

Dragon Ball Z has 38 filler episodes out of 291 total (13%). The watch path — manga canon, anime canon and mixed episodes — is 253 episodes.

Which Dragon Ball Z episodes can I skip?

You can skip these filler episodes: 9–10, 12–16, 39–43, 100, 102, 108–117, 124–125, 170–171, 174, 195–199, 202–203, 274, 288. Everything else is canon or mixed and worth watching. Use the checklist above to mark them off as skipped.

Is Dragon Ball Z filler worth watching?

Most DBZ filler can be safely skipped. One famous exception fans love is episode 125, "Goku's Ordeal" — it is pure filler (Goku and Piccolo learning to drive), but it is a beloved comedy episode.

Is Dragon Ball Z finished?

Dragon Ball Z is listed as: Complete. This page covers 291 episodes.
