Inuyasha filler FAQ

How many filler episodes does Inuyasha have?

Inuyasha has 35 filler episodes out of 193 total (18%). The watch path — manga canon, anime canon and mixed episodes — is 158 episodes.

Which Inuyasha episodes can I skip?

You can skip these filler episodes: 59, 63–65, 68, 72, 75–79, 89–101, 129–130, 133–134, 136–140, 162–163. Everything else is canon or mixed and worth watching. Use the checklist above to mark them off as skipped.

Is Inuyasha filler worth watching?

The episodes marked FILLER — SKIP on this page do not adapt the manga and do not affect the main story, so you can skip them without missing anything important. Episodes marked MIXED — WATCH blend canon and filler material and should be watched.

Is Inuyasha finished?

Inuyasha is listed as: Complete (incl. The Final Act). This page covers 193 episodes.
