Fairy Tail filler FAQ

How many filler episodes does Fairy Tail have?

Fairy Tail has 61 filler episodes out of 328 total (19%). The watch path — manga canon, anime canon and mixed episodes — is 267 episodes.

Which Fairy Tail episodes can I skip?

You can skip these filler episodes: 9, 19, 50, 70–75, 125–150, 202–226, 268. Everything else is canon or mixed and worth watching. Use the checklist above to mark them off as skipped.

Is Fairy Tail filler worth watching?

The episodes marked FILLER — SKIP on this page do not adapt the manga and do not affect the main story, so you can skip them without missing anything important. Episodes marked MIXED — WATCH blend canon and filler material and should be watched.
