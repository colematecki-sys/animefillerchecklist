Naruto filler FAQ

How many filler episodes does Naruto have?

Naruto has 90 filler episodes out of 220 total (41%). The watch path — manga canon, anime canon and mixed episodes — is 130 episodes.

Which Naruto episodes can I skip?

You can skip these filler episodes: 26, 97, 101–106, 136–140, 143–219. Everything else is canon or mixed and worth watching. Use the checklist above to mark them off as skipped.

Is Naruto filler worth watching?

The episodes marked FILLER — SKIP on this page do not adapt the manga and do not affect the main story, so you can skip them without missing anything important. Episodes marked MIXED — WATCH blend canon and filler material and should be watched.

Is Naruto finished?

Naruto is listed as: Complete. This page covers 220 episodes.
