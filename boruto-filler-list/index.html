Boruto filler FAQ

How many filler episodes does Boruto have?

Boruto has 28 filler episodes out of 293 total (10%). The watch path — manga canon, anime canon and mixed episodes — is 265 episodes.

Which Boruto episodes can I skip?

You can skip these filler episodes: 16–17, 40–41, 48–50, 67–69, 96–97, 112–119, 138–140, 152–154, 156, 256. Everything else is canon or mixed and worth watching. Use the checklist above to mark them off as skipped.

Why does Boruto have so much anime canon?

Most of Boruto was written for the anime rather than adapted from the manga, so it is classed as anime canon — original stories that fit the series continuity. Only 28 episodes are pure filler, and those are the only ones this guide marks as skippable.

Is Boruto finished?

Boruto is listed as: Complete. This page covers 293 episodes.
