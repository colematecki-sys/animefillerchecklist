One Piece filler FAQ

How many filler episodes does One Piece have?

One Piece has 94 filler episodes out of 1168 total (8%). The watch path — manga canon, anime canon and mixed episodes — is 1074 episodes.

Which One Piece episodes can I skip?

You can skip these filler episodes: 54–60, 98–99, 102, 131–143, 196–206, 220–225, 279–283, 291–292, 303, 317–319, 326–336, 382–384, 406–407, 426–429, 457–458, 492, 542, 575–578, 590, 626–627, 747–750, 780–782, 895–896, 907, 1029–1030. Everything else is canon or mixed and worth watching. Use the checklist above to mark them off as skipped.

Is One Piece filler worth watching?

Most One Piece filler can be skipped without missing anything. The big exception is the G-8 arc (episodes 196–206): it is filler, but it is widely considered a fan favorite and many fans recommend watching it anyway.
