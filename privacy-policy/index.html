Privacy Policy

This site is a static fan guide. It does not run user accounts and does not ask for or collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or passwords.

What is stored in your browser

The checklists save your progress (which episodes you have checked off) and your Spoiler Shield preference in your browser's local storage, on your own device. That data never leaves your device, is not sent to any server, and you can erase it at any time by clearing your browser's site data or using the "Reset progress" button on a show page.

Cookies and advertising

This site itself sets no tracking cookies. In the future the site may display advertising (for example Google AdSense) and use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are popular. If that happens, third-party ad partners may use cookies as described in their own policies, and this policy will be updated before ads go live.

Third-party links

Pages link to external reference sites such as Anime Filler List. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Request form

If you use the request form, your submission is handled by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, and delivered to us by email. If you include your optional email address, it is used only to reply to your request. Request data is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
